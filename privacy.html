Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VietDub Auto Studio collects, uses, stores and protects information when users access or use the application.

1. Information we collect

VietDub Auto Studio may process account profile information that users authorize through supported third-party platforms, such as an account identifier, display name and profile image.

The application may also process media files, captions and publishing settings selected by the user.

2. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate users through authorized platform login services.
  • Display the connected account inside the application.
  • Prepare and publish content requested by the user.
  • Maintain application security and functionality.
  • Diagnose errors and improve the service.

3. Third-party services

VietDub Auto Studio may integrate with third-party platforms through their official APIs and authorization systems. The use of information received from those platforms is subject to their applicable terms, policies and developer requirements.

4. User control

Users control which accounts they connect and which content they submit. Users may revoke access through the relevant third-party platform or request deletion of information associated with the application.

5. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested application functions, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain security.

6. Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

VietDub Auto Studio is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the connected social media platforms.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, legal requirements or platform integrations change. The effective date shown above will be updated accordingly.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: Privacy Policy - VietDub Auto Studio
